# MediTrack SRS Validation Checklist — v0.1

**Purpose:** Check whether the SRS is understandable, complete, consistent and suitable for the CityCare Clinic stakeholders and risks.

**Answer rule:** Yes = the SRS passes the question. No = a problem exists. N/A = the question does not apply. Add `?` when the answer is uncertain.

| ID | Item | Answer | Evidence | Where question came from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | Does each requirement describe one clear system obligation? | No | R-07 combines cancellation, schedule updating and receptionist notification in one requirement. | Good document rules: atomicity |
| C-02 | Does every performance requirement state a measurable limit with a number and unit? | No | R-02 says “quickly” but gives no response-time target. | Risk: slow booking, especially on busy Monday mornings |
| C-03 | Are there no contradictory requirements about appointment duration? | No | R-04 fixes every appointment at 15 minutes, while R-05 allows doctors to choose 10–30 minutes. | Good document rules: consistency; doctor workflow |
| C-04 | Are important terms used in the requirements clearly defined? | No | Terms such as “quickly”, “before”, “active appointments”, “normal working hours” and “clinic policy” are not defined. | Good document rules: clarity and unambiguity |
| C-05 | Can every requirement be verified using an objective pass/fail test? | No | R-02, R-08, R-09 and R-13 lack measurable or specific acceptance criteria. | Good document rules: testability |
| C-06 | Does R-03 specify how the system prevents two patients from receiving the same doctor/time slot? | No | R-03 only says double bookings will be prevented; it does not define slot locking or simultaneous booking behaviour. | Risk: double booking |
| C-07 | Does the SRS define how doctor availability is checked before a slot is booked? | No | R-03 does not state that a booking must be checked against the doctor’s available schedule. | Risk: invalid or overlapping appointments; doctor stakeholder |
| C-08 | Does the SRS specify when appointment reminders are sent? | No | R-06 says “before appointments” but gives no timing, such as 24 hours before. | Risk: patients forget appointments |
| C-09 | Does the SRS specify the channel used to send appointment reminders? | No | R-06 does not say whether reminders are sent by SMS, email, app notification or another channel. | Patient stakeholder; risk: missed reminders |
| C-10 | Does the SRS define any deadline or restriction for appointment cancellation? | No | R-07 says patients may cancel “at any time” without defining a cancellation cut-off or handling of late cancellations. | Patient and receptionist workflow; risk: schedule disruption |
| C-11 | Does the SRS identify which user roles may view patient medical records? | No | R-09 says records are stored securely but does not define role-based access. | Data protection officer stakeholder; risk: privacy breach |
| C-12 | Does the SRS specify an accessibility requirement for elderly patients? | No | R-08 says “user-friendly for all age groups” but gives no accessibility features or measurable standard. | Patient stakeholder; risk: elderly patients excluded |
| C-13 | Does the SRS explain how receptionists record emergency walk-in patients? | No | R-10 covers walk-in patients generally but does not mention emergency walk-ins or their priority. | Receptionist stakeholder; risk: emergency patients left out |
| C-14 | Does the SRS specify what information doctors can see in their daily schedule? | No | R-14 only says doctors can view the schedule; it does not identify the details shown, such as appointment time and patient identity. | Doctor stakeholder |
| C-15 | Does the SRS define the clinic administrator’s system responsibilities or permissions? | No | The administrator is listed as a user, but none of R-01–R-14 describes administrator functions. | Clinic administrator stakeholder; completeness |
| C-16 | Does the SRS define what counts as an “active appointment” for the three-appointment limit? | No | R-11 gives a limit of three but does not define whether pending, confirmed or rescheduled appointments count. | Patient stakeholder; clarity |
| C-17 | Does the SRS state the expected system capacity for busy periods such as Monday mornings? | No | R-02 has no response-time target and no requirement for peak booking volume or concurrent users. | Risk: Monday morning overload |
| C-18 | Does the SRS state the actual hours covered by “normal working hours”? | No | R-12 uses “normal working hours” without giving opening and closing times or days. | Patient and receptionist stakeholders; availability risk |
| C-19 | Does the SRS define or reference the clinic policy for overdue payments? | No | R-13 refers to “clinic policy” but the policy is not included or identified. | Risk: inconsistent payment handling; receptionist/administrator |
| C-20 | Does the SRS specify how long patient records are retained and when they are deleted? | No | R-09 gives no retention period or deletion rule. | Data protection officer stakeholder; privacy risk |
